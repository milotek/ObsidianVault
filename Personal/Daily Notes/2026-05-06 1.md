At a high level, how the Silk Web Inspector currently works is by replacing the port(s) for Silk with its own . This allows it to intercept all Silk messages sent between the web layer and the native client. The inspector already has a logger in place to record information about these messages . To implement the client emulator, this existing interception mechanism will be extended. Instead of just logging and forwarding messages, the emulator will selectively inject mock responses or events back to the web layer.
