I don't like making corporate "robo-posts", so I'll try keep things short. Today marked the end of my first week at Google as a Software Engineering Apprentice in London!

Never in a million years did I think I'd get this oppertunity - in the days coming up to decision day I noticed that I was literally losing sleep AND hair! 😭 I can't believe I get to work here!

Enough gushing... my first week has been lovely. I've really enjoyed meeting my fellow apprentices - they're great and supportive and us all getting to onboard together has been an experience. There's so much I wish I could tell you about (that I legally can't) that's going on at Google. They have crazy stuff - like a vending machine for laptops and headphones.

---

After just one week in the office, it's clear why 5,000+ people applied for this apprenticeship. Not only for the invaluable experience of working here, but...

> free breakfast, lunch, dinner

> free gym

> free (premium) laptops, headphones, monitors

> a makers lab

> the incredible people you'll meet

> having the G on your CV 😝

... and more. Of course, it's not truely free - I'll be working hard, learning and developing skills - but the culture you hear about this company is toted for a reason.

---

After 14 weeks learning in the Makers bootcamp, I'll be joining the Android dev team, working on the Google app for Android that billions of people use. 😳 Isn't that crazy?

And finally, after about 20,000 "congratulations!" from friends and family, nobody is more proud of me than my parents. Kanan Tekchandani and Ravi Tekchandani have had my back since day #1, and we've been through a lot together - but they never stopped supporting me. Love you guys ... #teksforever😸
