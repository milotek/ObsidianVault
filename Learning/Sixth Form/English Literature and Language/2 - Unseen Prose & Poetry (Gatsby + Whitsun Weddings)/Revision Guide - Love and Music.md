The theme of **love and music** is explored many times throughout both [Phillip Larkin](Phillip%20Larkin.md) and [Scott Fitzgerald](Scott%20Fitzgerald.md)'s work.

-----
## 3 points of connection 
Both Phillip and Fitzgerald explore the complexity of relationships, + concept of "false love", in the sense that love is not always straightforward, often depicting stagnant and/or rocky couples, such as within [Talking in Bed](Talking%20in%20Bed.md) *for Phillip* and [Tom and Daisy](Tom%20and%20Daisy.md) *for Fitzgerald.*

- [Talking in Bed](Talking%20in%20Bed.md) illustrates a couple with communications issues or alike, choosing to sweep issues within the unnamed relationship "under the rug".
