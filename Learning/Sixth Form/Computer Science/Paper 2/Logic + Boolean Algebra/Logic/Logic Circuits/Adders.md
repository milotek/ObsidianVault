Adders are a  [logic circuit](Logic%20Circuits.md) which outputs the result of the **number of inputs that are true**, and outputs that number in binary..

-----
## Variations
There are three main types of adders:

- [Half Adders](Half%20Adders.md)
- [Full Adders](Full%20Adders.md)
- And the much more complicated [Ripple Adder](Ripple%20Adder.md).
