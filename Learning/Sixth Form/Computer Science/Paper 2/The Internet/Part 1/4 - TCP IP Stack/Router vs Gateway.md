> [!TIP] Short Answer
> A gateway connects networks, while a router typically delivers data within a network.
> Historically, gateways and routers have been separate devices. However, it's becoming more common for their functions to be combined and simply called a router.

[Routers](Router.md) primarily direct traffic within and between networks, while [gateways](Gateway.md) act as entry points that connect different networks, often translating between different protocols.

And, a[ router](Router.md) typically manage traffic between similar networks (like LANs), whereas [gateways ](Gateway.md)connect dissimilar networks (like LANs to the internet or between different protocols).
